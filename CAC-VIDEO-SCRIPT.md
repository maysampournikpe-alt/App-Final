# Rumbo: the video (fast-cut, creator-style)

**Format:** vertical-feeling energy, but shot 16:9 for YouTube or Vimeo (set to Public). **Target length: 2:40. Hard limit: 3:00** (judges may penalize anything over).
**Style:** a quick-cut, story-first creator video. Hook in 3 seconds, a cut every 2 to 4 seconds, captions on every line, one running joke, one real moment, then the proof. You are the main character. The app is the plot twist.
**Voice:** talk fast and like a friend, not a presenter. Short sentences. Smile on the jokes.

Everything in [BRACKETS] is yours to fill in. Use your real story, real names and only true claims.

## Make it feel like a creator video (editing cheat sheet)

1. **Hook first.** The first 3 seconds are the joke and the problem, not your name. Your name comes at second 8.
2. **A cut every 2 to 4 seconds.** Never leave one shot on screen for more than 5 seconds, except the 988 moment on purpose.
3. **Captions on everything.** Big, bold, high-contrast. Many people watch on mute, and judges may too.
4. **Punch-in zooms** on important words ("source", "ask first", "988", "Report").
5. **Sound design.** A whoosh for every transition, a pop for every sticker, one beat that drops at the logo, silence for the crisis line.
6. **Running joke.** Pick one: the missed deadline text. It opens and closes the video.
7. **Show, don't tell.** Every claim ("real programs", "right topic") gets a screen recording right then.
8. **Real face, real room.** Film in your actual school, house or desk. Natural light beats a fancy set.
9. **Sped-up screen recordings** (1.5x to 2x), but slow down on the part you want judges to read.
10. **No copyrighted music or clips.** Use royalty-free music you have the right to use (YouTube Audio Library, or music you made). Only film people who said yes, and get a parent's permission for anyone under 18 who appears.
