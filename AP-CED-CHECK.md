# AP outline check (October 23, 2026)

Rumbo's AP units and topics are an outline of each course. This file records what was checked against the College Board's public AP Central pages and secondary summaries on this date, what was changed, and what still needs a person to compare against the official Course and Exam Description (CED) PDF.

**How it was checked:** web searches that return AP Central page text and prep-site summaries. The CED PDFs themselves could not be opened from the build environment, so topic titles inside a unit could not be compared word for word. A unit list or a "what changed" statement from AP Central counts as verified. A claim that only a prep site made counts as unverified.

**Rules to remember:** Rumbo writes its own questions and notes. It uses only the public unit and topic names. It never copies College Board text or released questions.

## Changed in this pass

| Course | What changed | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AP Statistics | Rebuilt from 9 units to the redesigned 5 units for 2026-27 (May 2027 exam). Removed analyzing departures from linearity, combining random variables, the geometric distribution, chi-square goodness of fit, and inference for slopes. | AP Physics C: Mechanics | Now 7 units: Kinematics, Force and Translational Dynamics, Work Energy and Power, Linear Momentum, Torque and Rotational Dynamics, Energy and Momentum of Rotating Systems, Oscillations. | AP Central CED (effective Fall 2024) |

| AP English Language | Removed two "units" that are not in the CED (Multiple-Choice Strategies, Conventions and Revision). The CED does not title its nine units. It uses four big ideas, which the first four units here follow, and three essay types. | CED text |

## Still needs a person to check against the CED PDF

1. **Topic titles inside units.** Unit lists are right. Exact topic wording and counts could not be compared for any course. AP Statistics is the most likely to differ: sources say Unit 1 has 13 topics, and Rumbo has 17.
2. **AP Statistics sampling distributions.** Rumbo places the central limit theorem in Unit 2 and the sampling distributions of proportions and means at the start of Units 3 and 4. Confirm that against the CED.
3. **World languages (Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Spanish).** Revised for 2026-27 (project, digital exam). The unit and theme names are the older six themes, so check them.
4. **Exam times for some courses.** Calculus (45 or 42 questions), AP Statistics minutes, and the physics 42-question format for 2027 came from conflicting sources.
5. **AP Research** has no AP Exam (it is graded on a paper and presentation), and **AP Seminar** has a written exam. Practice questions for these test the skills, not an exam.
6. **New 2026-27 courses not in Rumbo:** AP Business with Personal Finance, AP Cybersecurity, AP 2-D Art and Design, AP 3-D Art and Design, AP Drawing. Add them once someone checks their units against the CEDs.
7. **Existing AI-written questions saved in the shared bank** under the old AP Statistics, Physics C and Chemistry unit ids are no longer served, because they do not match the new units. New questions are written from the updated outline.
